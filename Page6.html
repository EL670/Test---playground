We all have to make decisions as basic and immediate as: How do I stay healthy? How do I take care of my loved ones who may live far away when I’m in lockdown? Is it okay to go outside? Can I let my children play outdoors? And then there’s trying to plan for the future: What will happen to my job? When will things return to normal? What will the new normal look like? I won’t pretend to provide definitive answers to any of these questions and note that the very nature of the information we’re seeing speaks to cultural norms and differences.
